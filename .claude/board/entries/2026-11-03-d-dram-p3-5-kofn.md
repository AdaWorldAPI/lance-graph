# D-DRAM-P3-5: k-of-n on the shipped carry words loses to facade byte counters

**Status:** MEASURED-LAB, kill condition fired. Owner: this session (plan `.claude/plans/d-dram-p3-integrated-v1.md`, IDEAS "k-of-n on the shipped carry words"). Probe: `crates/crepe-plan-probe/examples/kofn_carry_words.rs`.

## What was measured

The question: per lane, are at least k of n masks set? The arms:
- **carry**: the Abakus register fold, built from the two shipped words only. `full_add_mask` adds two masks into column 0, `half_add_mask` ripples the carry upward, and a constant-k comparator runs over the planes.
- **simd**: native counting on the same facade, one `U8x64` of counters per word, `saturating_add(mask_blend(m, 0, 1))` per mask, then `cmpgt_mask(k - 1)`.
- **lane** and **bytes**: naive per-lane counting.
- **or** and **and**: plain Boolean folds.
- **oracle**: walks set bits with `trailing_zeros` into per-lane `u32` counts.

Setup: 1024 words (65,536 lanes), median of 31 repetitions, three processes. One host with AVX-512 and no VNNI, built for the default x86-64 target. The facade backend that build selected was not recorded.

## Result (ns per 64-lane word, ranges over 3 processes)

| workload | n | simd | carry | carry / simd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dense p=7/8 | 3 | 2.8–5.0 | 7.0–13 | 1.7–2.6× |
| dense p=7/8 | 8 | 6.4–6.8 | 15–16 | 2.2–2.4× |
| dense p=7/8 | 32 | 24–25 | 61–65 | 2.4–2.6× |
| random p=1/2 | 3 | 2.6–4.2 | 6.8–7.8 | 1.7–2.9× |
| random p=1/2 | 8 | 6.2–6.8 | 18–20 | 2.8–3.1× |
| random p=1/2 | 32 | 24–38 | 58–100 | 2.3–4.0× |
| sparse one lane | 3 | 2.6–3.0 | 6.7–7.6 | 2.3–2.9× |
| sparse one lane | 8 | 6.1–6.7 | 13–15 | 1.9–2.3× |
| sparse one lane | 32 | 24–43 | 87–130 | 3.0–3.7× |

Each row covers k = 1, ⌈n/2⌉ and n; the ratio range is taken over all three. The carry arm is slower than facade counting in every cell, dense masks included. The naive lane loop (130–1,300 ns) and byte loop (33–260 ns) are slower than both. The plain OR/AND fold is 2.0–23 ns.

**Correctness:** in all 3 runs, every arm agrees with the oracle in every cell. The silence twin holds: k = 1 equals OR and k = n equals AND exactly. Anti-vacuity: on random masks the middle k splits the lanes (density between 5% and 95%).

**Disable runs** (commit first, then patch, then `git checkout`; each patch asserted to apply):
- the comparator drops its `gt` term: 21 FAIL lines;
- the ripple stops after one column: 29 FAIL lines;
- restored: 0.

## Reading

The kill condition named in IDEAS fired: on dense masks the carry-word threshold is slower than native. The finding is narrow. Built from the two shipped `u64` words, a bit-sliced counter does not beat a byte counter that the facade already has, for a k-of-n threshold up to n = 32. The likely reason is width. The shipped words act on one `u64` (64 lanes) per call, while `U8x64` counts 64 lanes in one vector add. This is HYPOTHESIS, not measured: no wide carry-word arm was built.

This does not contradict #402. There, the bit-plane counter beat 64 scalar per-lane adds; here the comparison arm is a vector byte counter.

## Coverage against the #1949 A-gates (proposal by a GPT session; adopted after review)

- **A1, partial:** n ∈ {3, 8, 32}, k ∈ {1, ⌈n/2⌉, n}. Not covered: k = 0, k = n + 1, and n ∈ {1, 2, 5, 16, 24}.
- **A2, partial:** dense, random and one-lane masks. Not covered: zero, full, alternating, tail masks; only one host and target.
- **A6, partial:** a misplaced carry is caught by the oracle (disable 2).
- **A5 and A7:** not covered. No NULL/Bag semantics; an isolated microkernel only, with no Quack lowering and no other ISA tier.

## Open

- A carry fold over `U64x8` (512 lanes per call) would need a wide adder. That word does not exist, and building one would be a new ndarray word that needs its owner (#1949 §7). It is not built here.
- Repeat with `target-cpu=native` and on AVX2-only and NEON hosts. The facade backend selected is unrecorded.
- The model may still win where byte counters cannot follow: counts above 255 (more than 8 planes), and folds that keep the planes for later arithmetic. Neither was measured.
