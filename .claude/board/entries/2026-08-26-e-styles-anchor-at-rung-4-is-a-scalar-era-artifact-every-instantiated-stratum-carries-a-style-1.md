### E-STYLES-ANCHOR-AT-RUNG-4-IS-A-SCALAR-ERA-ARTIFACT-EVERY-INSTANTIATED-STRATUM-CARRIES-A-STYLE-1

**Operator-ruled 2026-08-26** (session review, same arc as the #1049 tower
reading). ⊘ Scope-corrects `E-RUNG-CONTENT-LADDER-1`'s rung-4 row — the row
stands as a statement about VOCABULARY; its ANCHORING is regraded.

**The ruling.** Placing the thinking-style macros (`StyleFamily`, the
autopoiesis triangle) *at* rung 4 dates from the scalar-rung era: one
`RungLevel` position, no alpha layer, "rung was one bit for everything" — the
model of the world in which the (since NOT-CANONICAL) 1033 plan was written.
Under the tower reading (grounding-descent plan §7f: strata each holding a
live model of the stratum below, in parallel), **ten rung instances exist,
and none of them may be operationally idle just because the old model had a
single active position.**

Consequence, stated as the new demarcation:

- **The content ladder stays a hierarchy of OBJECTS** — observations at 0–1,
  the 144 verb atoms at 2, the 34 NARS recipes at 3. What kind of thing
  occupies a stratum is still ordinal.
- **Style is not a floor. Style is a per-stratum AXIS.** Every instantiated
  stratum carries its own operating mode (frozen templates × learned revision
  × exploration — the triangle), selected per stratum, not once for the
  tower. `StyleFamily` remains the vocabulary; "rung 4" was its era-bound
  address, not its type.
- **Selection and outcome are two roles, not one.** The style is selected
  per stratum from problem-texture RESONANCE (the prior: which style should
  probably fit); that stratum's ΔF is the OUTCOME channel — it reinforces,
  revises, or reopens the selection, it never selects directly. Deriving the
  style from the ΔF band alone would turn the feedback signal back into a
  small global control rule and erase the original architecture's selection
  intelligence. FROZEN / LEARNED / EXPLORE are three response regimes whose
  switching is qualified by outcome, not three bands of a ΔF scale.

**What this does NOT reopen:** the persona-36 demarcation is untouched (the
adjective styles remain the separate, unwired persona storyline — this entry
is about `StyleFamily`, the rung-4 macro side only). O6's "triangle structure
unbuilt" stays open and now has its shape: the composed macro object is
per-stratum, indexed by the INSTANTIATED mask.

**Falsifier for whoever builds it:** a tower with two instantiated strata must
be able to hold two DIFFERENT styles simultaneously and switch one without
touching the other; a design where selecting a style sets tower-global state
has rebuilt the scalar era and fails this entry.
