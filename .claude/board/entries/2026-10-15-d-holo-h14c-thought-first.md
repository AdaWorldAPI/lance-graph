- Acceptance A–E met: a read consults exactly the requested lane tables
  (counter == k for U = 2, 8, 31); at k = 2 the read at U = 31 costs
  0.90–1.01× (hot) and 0.95–1.16× (cold) the read at U = 2; populations load
  no value; no row is built outside the terminal; every rewrite scenario
  returns the requested coordinates or refuses.
- C vs B on query-shaped reads is mixed: B faster hot at ≤ 1 % dirty (one row
  index vs k presence tests); C faster cold from 10 % at k ≤ 8; B faster at
  k ≥ 16 in most cells. Sparse binary search is slowest.
- Positional tables are resident at about one page per touched page: uniform
  1 % costs ≈ 955 KiB per table for ≈ 10 KiB of values (flagged, every T);
  clustered 1 % costs 62 KiB. Seal is page-fault bound at low density.
- Lance 13 prediction holds: a uniform 1 % mask reads the same bytes as no
  mask (ratio 1.00) while rows scanned drop to 692 of 65,536; clustered 1 %
  reads 0.24×. Materialization style changed bytes in 0 of 120 cells.
- A stale coordinate map gives silent omission, not wrong coordinates;
  stable row ids keep `_rowid` across update and compaction (0 moved), not
  across overwrite. The version check refuses all six stale cases.

**OPEN:** bounding positional-table residency without traversal; stable row
ids for lane datasets; page-local presence for scattered masks. No hardware
counters on this VM (cache/TLB measured indirectly).
