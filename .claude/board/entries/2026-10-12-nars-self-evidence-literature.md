## First-hand research

- Wang, Li & Hammer (2018), _Self in NARS_: distinct internal inference traces, attention events and original evidence; preference for disjoint-evidence revision over duplicate evidence. Functional satisfaction and appraisal are discussed, not a demonstration of subjective qualia.
- Jøsang (2007), _Cumulative and Averaging Fission of Beliefs_: cumulative combination for independent, averaging for dependent evidence, and inverse fission subject to stated models. Not automatically NARS revision.
- Goertzel (2021), _Patterns of Cognition_: metagraph fold/unfold theorem assumes stable graph during the fold, and its decomposition requires associativity conditions. Merely immutable IDs do not ensure snapshot-stable reads.

## Probes to consider, not authorized

- A repeated observation in two hypothesis layers may **not** raise F/C merely by being revisited; two independent observations inside one layer must still count twice.
- Ablation removing one contributor must recover the remaining evidence, or refuse exact retraction if the overlap and weight are not known.
- An `AlphaStamp` for attention cannot replace an evidence-base ID or derivation receipt; same truth with different derivations must remain traceable.
- A pinned Lance version k must replay identically while k+1 appends, before any mathematical "static-fold equivalence" claim.
