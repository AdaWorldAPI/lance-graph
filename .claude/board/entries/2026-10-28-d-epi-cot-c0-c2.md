# D-EPI-COT C0–C2: substrate audit, derivation ledger and load-bearing ablation

**Status:** TEST-PINNED (C1/C2), MEASURED (C0 audit). Code: `crates/lance-graph-planner/src/nars/support.rs`. Audit: `docs/research/epistemic-cot/c0-substrate-audit.md`.

## Findings (C0)

- None of the 34 contract recipe kernels calls a NARS truth function, builds a statement, or reads a source, stamp, Epi5 state or chain. `ThoughtCtx` holds scalars, `Vec<f32>` candidates and `(topic, f, c)` tuples. `KernelMaturity::Operational` (31 of 34) means only "changes `ThoughtCtx`". Under F9 all 34 fail by construction.
- The five planner tactics (`nars/tactics.rs`, recipe ids 4, 6, 7, 8, 11) are the only term-logic implementations; they are the base for the first vertical slice.
- `BeliefArena` keeps one derivation per statement and gives derived entries an empty stamp, so alternative chains and their source roots are lost; it has no retraction.
- `TruthValue` fixes the evidence horizon at k = 1. `NarsInference::Synthesis` is an arithmetic mean, not a NAL law. Pearl `Intervention` / `Counterfactual` map to the abduction truth function.
- `CertificationModel` has no UNKNOWN outcome (missing counts as negative), no effect size, no sample-size or uncertainty bound, and no multiple-comparison correction (`associated` tries up to five populations). Simpson stops at `Related`.
- Recipe eligibility from Epi5 exists only in the lab (`cognitive-shader-driver/examples/shared/affordance_law.rs`).

## Decision (C1/C2)

- `DerivationLedger` beside `BeliefArena`, which is unchanged: append-only observations `(stmt, truth, source)` and admitted derivations `(chain, law, premises, conclusion)`.
- Admission checks the step's terms against its law's figure (the figures the planner tactics use); `admit` takes a tactic `Candidate` directly. Invalid figures are refused (L8).
- Evaluation under an `Ablation`: proofs over pooled premise evidence; pooled truth = the root-disjoint proof set with the most total evidence weight; the chains that still derive the statement.
- Two reads: `load()` (existence: FirstCritical / SecondCritical / Synergistic / Redundant / NotLoadBearing / Absent) and `sensitivity()` (Δfrequency, Δconfidence, chains and roots lost).
- Bounds refuse: `Refusal::{Steps, Depth, Pool}`. Evidence horizon stays k = 1.
- Derivation dependence only. Outcome and trigger dependence, causation and Epi5 writes are out of scope; load-bearing is evidence for an existing Epi5 reading, never a new bit.

## Evidence

16 tests; 10 disables, each red. F10: conclusion f 0.81 → 0.72 with chain B's independent support, premise contradiction 0.85, original replays exactly. L6: the conclusion survives, `load` says NotLoadBearing, and Δf < −0.3, Δc < −0.5. Two earlier versions were wrong: pooling only at the conclusion left F10's truth unmoved, and greedy selection by single-proof confidence could drop two independent proofs that outweigh one shared-root proof.
