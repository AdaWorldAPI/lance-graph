(`lance-graph-r2il/src/polyfill.rs`). One function, `FoldDialect::call`, dispatches all 17 opcodes

and `admit`, with no preparation or execution (two r2il integration tests).

**Finding:** the trace code → opcode → word → method works for one lens and two existing test
bindings, at arm granularity: a change at `lib.rs:980` reaches word 5 (`IntSLess`) and both methods
that use it. The first two hops are source structure; the third is DT64 registration. With the
dispatch mapping of one opcode removed, its word is UNKNOWN and the other rows are unchanged; a
witness taken on another revision of the source gives no answer.

**Limits:** `realizes` is an arm reading, not a compiler fact and not a behavioural witness. At
symbol level one function realizes every word. The native lowering path is not mapped. No
production crate registers a binding with DO effects; Foundry's `can_cook_method` is ON + WHEN only.
