## Falsifiers named, not yet written

- `NoDuplicatedSourceRead`: after an exact storage predicate, a counter on
  source scans and predicate evaluations shows no second read downstream.
- `NoDuplicatedSelectionOwnership`: every view borrows from the one binding;
  an owned second survivor or source array fails.

Neither plan has a file called a falsifier register; these two are recorded
here and in §1a.1 until W10 lists them.
