# 2026-09-25 — `Keep` over a ≤3-plane Boolean chain folds to one ternlog pass

**Status:** MEASURED · OPEN (the remaining 1.6× to the inlined ceiling, attributed to the ndarray kernel's loop shape — HYPOTHESIS; 4–5 planes)
**Supersedes:** the "Fold `Keep` for ≤3 planes" line under Open in `entries/2026-09-25-llvm-whole-stack-fold-ceiling.md`.

## What landed (mask-risc only)
- `Program::fused_keep()` recognises the same symbolic chain as `fused_ternlog()`, with `Terminal::Keep` as the consumer. The chain interpreter is shared (`ternlog_table`), not copied.
- `Lowering::TernlogKeep(FusedKeep)` is tried after `Ternlog`. `execute_compiled` takes it only when the caller passes `Out::Mask`. It makes one `ternlog_dispatch` call from the borrowed planes straight into `out`, and writes no slot.
- The tiled path's write rules are kept exactly:
  - a cut edge word (unaligned `lo`, or unaligned `hi < n_rows`) is merged under `edge_mask`, so the neighbour extent's bits survive;
  - the population's last word, when reached whole, is written whole with its dead tail cleared, as the tiled ternlog arm leaves it.
- With `Out::None`, a whole-width scratch caller still reads the result from its slot, so that shape stays tiled. `Compiled::requires_scratch` / `Program::requires_scratch` stay `true` for these programs.

## Measured (`examples/llvm_fold_probe.rs`, n = 2^20, `target-cpu=native`)

| chain | Keep tiled | Keep fused | Keep inlined (lab ceiling) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `(a&b)\|!c` | 17.4 µs | 8.0 µs | 4.9 µs |
| `((a^b)&!c)\|(a&c)` | 19.6 µs | 8.0 µs | 5.1 µs |

The fused arm is asserted word-for-word equal to the tiled arm, which is forced past the fold by a slot at `FUSED_SLOT_CAP`.

## Gates
`tests/keep_fold.rs` (6 tests):
- fused vs tiled over random ≤3-plane chains, including odd immediates, whole and partial extents, and an `out` prefilled with garbage;
- fused vs `reference_execute`;
- a zero-slot scratch suffices, and carved slots stay zero;
- the last word's tail is cleared;
- a 4-plane chain declines;
- `Out::None` stays tiled.

Disable runs (committed first, then restored; each went red):

| disable | tests failing |
|---|---|
| edge merge overwrites the neighbour's bits | 1 |
| no dead-tail clear | 3 |
| `TernlogKeep` never chosen | 4 |
| head edge word skipped | 1 |

Correction along the way: the first version of the scratch-untouched test poisoned a buffer and then carved it. `Scratch::over` zero-fills its carve, so the poison could never survive, whatever path ran. It now reads every carved slot after the run and requires zeros; the tiled path would leave the kept mask in slot 1.

## Reading
- The fold buys 2.2–2.5×. It stops at 1.6× from the ceiling. The Count fold, through the same dispatch, stops at 1.16×, so per-call overhead is not the gap.
- HYPOTHESIS, not measured: the remainder is `ndarray::simd::mask_ternlog`'s loop shape. It does one `U64x8` per iteration with no unroll, plus a store, where the lab loop is free to unroll. Closing that is an ndarray kernel PR, not a mask-risc change.
