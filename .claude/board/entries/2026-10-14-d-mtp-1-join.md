`.claude/plans/metta-polyfill-v1.md` §6a (pre-registration) and §6b (results). Probe

**Outcome:**
- `(match &kb (, (parent $x $y) (parent $y $z)) ($x $z))` lowers to
  `Plane(valid) AND Semijoin { fk: dst, foreign: valid }` → one mask-risc program
  (`Gather`); pairs are emitted only over the kept bits. `lance-graph-relalg` has no hop,
  so the plan's "through relalg" was corrected to Quack.
- Pairs == datafrog (fork) at n = 10²..10⁶ on a total (F1) and a partial (F2) relation;
  counts == Hyperon and PeTTa. Two disable runs red exactly as pre-registered.
- At 10⁶: emission 4.0–5.7 ns/pair against datafrog 59–71 ns/pair; with ingest charged
  per emitted pair, 6.5–7.1 ns (F1) and 8.6–9.2 ns (F2, 666,666 facts for 476,190 pairs). The folded count is 1.2–1.8 ns/row, not the sub-nanosecond the plan
  expected: the cost is the dependent random read of the gather, not the mask algebra.
