# D-HOLO-H14d — logic → address mask → masked read → terminal, C only (v4, 2026-10-16)

**Status:** MEASURED. Plan: `.claude/plans/d-holo-h14d-logic-mask-read-v1.md` (pre-registered before the run; amendments A1, A2 before any timing; results there).
**Probe:** `crates/holo-h14d-probe` (excluded from the workspace). `tier: v4 (avx512f)`, rustc 1.99.0, lance 13.0.0 (resolved at build time and printed by the probe; no lockfile is tracked). Measured commit `802db7df`.

- Stage 1 (relalg logic): of 2,000 generated programs, 675 decided
  symbolically with 0 programs run, 396 answered only after the rewrite (as
  written they exceed the 8 scratch slots), 2 widened by it (absorption missed
  after De Morgan; `ISS-RELALG-ABSORPTION-AFTER-DE-MORGAN`). On the 1,583
  answered both ways: 1,065 passes rewritten vs 5,898 as written. 1.9 µs per
  program to reduce.
- Stage 2 (address mask `R`): 2–10 ns per 64-address word (0.03–0.15 ns per
  address), growing with operands, not with `|R|`; flat in density within
  1.25× in 11 of 14 rows (three single-cell spikes).
- Stage 3 (Lance 13 prefilter with `R`): rows scanned == returned == `|R|` in
  all 96 cells. Bytes follow page locality: full tile uniform 1 % reads 1.00×
  (H14c reproduced), clustered 1 % 0.19×, clustered 10 % 0.99× (64-address runs
  are below Lance page size); a 1,024- or 8,192-address aperture reads
  0.01–0.12×; an empty `R` reads 0 bytes.
- Stage 4 (terminals): payload reads == `|R| × k` exactly; about 6 ns per lane
  load per survivor hot, plus a fixed scan of `R`'s 1,024 words (~1.5 ns each),
  so per-survivor cost is not flat at small `|R|` (prediction 4 falsified as
  stated). Fused Σ: about 1.3 ns per summed value at 50 %, ~5× under the
  explicit terminal. Single-address query: 8–45 ns hot, 17–184 ns cold.

**OPEN:** page-scale clustering (which address order puts a thought's
survivors on few Lance pages); the payload terminal's fixed word scan; the
relalg absorption miss. No hardware counters on this VM.
