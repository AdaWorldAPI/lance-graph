# D-DT64-REACH-1: a witnessed reach over a lent relation (2026-11-18)

**Status:** MEASURED. Answers EXO-331 option 2. Follows the pair plane and the
relation socket (#2126, #2127) and the cube lending its planes (#2131).

`crates/lance-graph-dt64/src/relation.rs`:

- `relation_reach(source, rel, frame, dir, seed, target, buf)`: the positions
  reachable from `seed` along one relation, every step landing in `target`
  (all positions when absent), seeds included. The relation is borrowed once
  through `RelationSource` under the reader's frame. The answer goes into
  caller-owned buffers (`ReachBuf`: reached mask, per-position hop count,
  scratch). The function has no allocation in it.
- `reach_path(...)`: one path to a reached position, read back from the hop
  counts by looking each step up in the relation again. A witness the relation
  no longer holds is `WitnessMismatch`, never read as a path.
- Refused before anything is written: a relation the owner does not hold, a
  frame the plane was not built under, a plane whose two axes differ, any
  buffer of the wrong length.

`crates/lance-graph-xlog/src/cube.rs`: `Cube::reach` no longer builds edge
rows per call; it runs `relation_reach` over the cube's own plane. It still
allocates its masks and scratch, because `SetExpr` evaluation returns owned
masks. A consumer that needs the allocation-free path calls `relation_reach`
on the cube directly, with `table.seal()` as the frame.

Tests: dt64 `tests/lane_socket.rs` module `relation_reach` (4): mask and hop
of every position equal a breadth-first walk over the edge list, forward and
backward, with and without a target, from dirty buffers, on 512 positions with
a chain, a branch, a cycle, an island and 150 random edges; every path is made
of held edges; a stale witness and unreached / out-of-axis positions are
refused; every refusal leaves the buffers untouched. xlog `tests/body.rs` (1):
the same reach taken over a `Cube`, after a logged add and retire, with an old
witness and an old frame (after `rebase`) refused. The existing reach tests of
the cube are unchanged and pass.

Disables, 7 of 7 red: target ignored; reached positions re-entered; square
check off; path takes any predecessor; hop not cleared; hop length unchecked;
seed hop not zero.

Cost (`cargo run --release -p lance-graph-dt64 --example reach_cost`, native
AVX-512, one VM, median of 41, each answer asserted against the walk), ns per
call from one seed:

| positions | edges | reached | rounds | ns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256 | 341 | 2 | 2 | 48 |
| 256 | 1,109 | 251 | 8 | 985 |
| 1,024 | 1,365 | 422 | 46 | 3,583 |
| 1,024 | 4,437 | 1,007 | 8 | 4,978 |
| 4,096 | 5,461 | 1,888 | 41 | 35,774 |
| 4,096 | 17,749 | 4,038 | 9 | 58,259 |

Cost grows with reached positions times words per row (the row or), so it is
quadratic in the axis for a dense reach.

Open:
- Hop counts are `u16`; a reach deeper than 65,534 hops is refused
  (`HopOverflow`).
- `hop` / `any` in Quack `Draft` (EXO-331 option 1) is not done.
- The OGIT allowed-edge parity run (2,948 rows) is the requester's to run.
- `Cube::closure` still builds its own rows and is limited to 64 positions.
- A plane's rows are dense: `positions² / 8` bytes per direction (2 MiB at
  4,096 positions).
