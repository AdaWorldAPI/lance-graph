# 2026-11-11 — CG-BEH-2: ON, WHEN and DO read as separate registered facts

**Status:** MEASURED (lab). `crates/cg-beh-probe`, `.claude/probes/2026-11-11-cg-beh-2/`. Owner EXO-155; board `P-EXO-18`.

A read-only lab crate reads a method's ON positions, WHEN guard and ordered DO steps from a
`BindStore` (`method_ref`, `admit`, `resolve`), for its own class and for Foundry's `CanCook`, and
compares two such readings. 23 tests; 15 of 15 disable runs red.

**Findings.**
- A guard change does not move the binding digest; only `admit`'s witness comparison catches it.
- `admit` takes no `ClassView`. Over a reordered basis of the same count `resolve` refuses and
  `admit` still admits the old reference.
- Registration does not check guard positions against the basis or the reads.
- `on` is registered and never evaluated by dt64, r2il, xlog or the Foundry lab.
- `CanCook` (16 methods) has no DO step, so no polyfill word implementation reaches it through DO.

**OPEN.** Executed parity per word. Source attribution for ON and WHEN. Whether the guard belongs in
the binding digest and whether `admit` should see the basis are owner questions for EXO-155, not
decided here. `.mermaid/05` and `/06` still describe PR #1996 as open (it is merged).
