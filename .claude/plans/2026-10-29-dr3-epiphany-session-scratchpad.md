## 0. North star, with strictly separated meanings

**Question:** Can an existing source-witnessed DR64³ relational/focus representation plus Thought-Git's 64 sparse hypothesis slots provide a *losslessly recoverable* structured residual learning substrate, on which a read-only `Predicate64 × Thought64 × CausalEdge64` projection learns to prioritize **independently verifiable** epistemic breakthroughs, without hallucinating truth or copying neighbor features?

- DR64³ is the intended *native logical relational focus*. In `d-native-repr-contract-v1.md`, S9 is **LAB** DR64² (`&[u64;64]`); S10, the third axis, is **PROPOSED/UNRESOLVED** (NFR-OP-2). In `dr256-where-capstone-v1.md` Amendment A, *relation ordinal* is a **working model**, not a ratified type. No mandated dense cube.
- Predicate64 means **≤64 source-bound typed predicate ordinals within a focus**, not CE64's P=8-bit palette index and not a global ontology.
- Thought64 is a **64-slot budget**: `t=0` sealed givens, `t=1..63` speculative branches; `t=64` **invalid**. CE64's `W` field (bits 53..58) is a **thought breadcrumb** under the 2026-10-17 O3 owner decision, written on merge/materialization. A borrowed unchanged givens CE64 may still carry W=0 while read in thought t>0; therefore **do not demand W==t on every merged read**. Check W==writer-slot only where the producer explicitly materialized that thought, with generation/frame provenance. After a Lance seal W is not an authoritative thought identifier. The older `edge.rs` / `layout.rs` documentation calls W a corpus-root handle: it is superseded in the owner decision but still a code/doc migration hazard. A thought ordinal never substitutes for source-root evidence.
- CausalEdge64 is a **typed 64-bit word**, not 64 independent causal relations. Interpret with the class/rail/generation/provenance: S/P/O, NARS frequency and confidence, Pearl causal mask, orientation, activation/inference reading, plasticity/maturation reading, six-bit W, Epi5 upper five bits. CausalEdgeV3-96 is a distinct type.
- Two **alternative interpretations** require an owner choice, not silent equivalence:
  - **A (first falsifier):** each (predicate, thought) yields **one** CE64 word, whose 64 bit positions are optionally a bit-sliced read: 64×64×64 bits = **32 KiB raw dense word payload**; semantics must be decoded by fields.
  - **B (different proposal):** 64 **edge-ordinal objects** for every (predicate, thought), each a 64-bit CE64 = **2 MiB raw dense payload**, requiring a fourth semantic role/edge identity. No owner decision, so defer B rather than pretending it is A.
- "Exact" independently means (i) correct identity/frame/focus/scope routing; (ii) exact reconstruction of declared payload bits or checked fixed-point data; and (iii) declared numerical loss/gradient parity. Neither implies the next.
- The numerical `ndarray::hpc::codec9` / DR512 uses one never-touched codeword and 511 numeric values. TurboVec-U9 uses **512 numeric Lloyd-Max centroids**. Do NOT re-use 9-bit words across these contracts. The former transports presence/signed exact codes; the latter is an **approximate search candidate generator**.
- More thoughts ≠ more sources. **Root-disjoint evidence only** may increase evidential support. `Load::Synergistic` detects dependence of a *derivation*, not causal proof or an automatic epiphany. A trained predictor may only reorder investigation candidates.
- No mutable ANGLE, no second ledger, no direct source/value gathers after binding, no witness laundering, no NARS/CE64/Epi5 writes from a model prediction, no forced merges, no force push.
