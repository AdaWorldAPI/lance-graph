**Four non-interchangeable clocks:** (i) Atlas neural events, spike timing, eligibility, physiological time; (ii) fixed-candidate synaptic activation/deactivation and separately qualified morphology/myelination; (iii) epistemic F/C, source-root revisions and Epi5 certification; (iv) metacognitive policy/tactic/attention adaptation scored on held-out consequences. An optional fifth view is human narrative time/reading order; it must not be silently identified with neural time.

**Epistemic restraint:** a simulated spike is not a NARS truth value; a Moore neighbor is not an actual synapse; XLOG reachability does not prove neural propagation or causal certification; a THOUGHT prediction is not a fresh observation; a qualia label is not a demonstrated phenomenal experience.

## 4. The questions that make these layers *one* research program

**Go board:** Raumgewinn proposes a local candidate; Einfluss forecasts reach; Infight exhibits competition; Staunen detects informative boundary; XLOG reads variations; episodic witness remembers the move sequence; Mistaber keeps incompatible interpretations honest; OpenPsi/Flow allocates effort; H9 tracks expectation/evidence; H10 checks improvement; H11 compares precommitted THOUGHTs with played stones; H12 detects overconfidence; H13 measures the qualitative trajectory, including its relation to human reports.

**Language/child:** DeepNSM-v2 ordered SPO + the Markov context of a story; maintain Broca/Wernicke as a **network analogy**, not two isolated biological modules. Preserve interpretation at time t and retrospective reinterpretation after a twist (wolf carries child, later known as rescue); test text order swaps, attribution, hallucinated cause, duplicated story root. Do not overwrite episode 3 with its later explanation.

**Mammals/platypus:** Mistaber inheritance and source-qualified overrides distinguish default/live-bearing generalization from an egg-laying mammal, without erasing taxonomy, confusing exception with negation, or turning a copied report into new confidence. Kahneman/Tversky heuristics motivate cheap approximate policies and explicit bias controls, not a second truth law.

**Music / guitar tuner:** measured tones→phase/chord context→progression/anticipation→candidate anonymous qualitative geometry→human reported affect (e.g. GEMS/EMMA) as external validation. The 219 musical sidecars are archival sources, not an inference proof; use held-out raters, temporal shuffle and neutral-label baselines.

**Atlas neural observatory (future, read-only):** pinned AtlasIR event stream→actual synaptic geometry/temporal raster/population signals→visualization of prediction vs observed and learned vs unchanged trajectories→optionally qualified music/qualia projection. EEG-like brainwaves need a scientifically declared forward/population model; do not synthesize them from neuron positions. Respect physical time; replay must not reapply effects. This instrument is a proposed visualization, not proof of learning or consciousness.
