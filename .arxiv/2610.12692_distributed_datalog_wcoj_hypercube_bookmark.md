## Reported in the indexed paper abstract

The authors study distributed-memory recursive Datalog evaluation with **worst-case optimal joins** (WCOJ), particularly cyclic multi-way joins that can produce very large binary-join intermediates.

Their approach uses **HyperCube partitioning** with variable-aligned process-grid dimensions. Iterative semi-naive execution repartitions and re-replicates newly derived tuples while retaining a shared layout for local WCOJ intersections. The system uses MPI collectives; the abstract reports scalability results on Argonne's Aurora and Polaris supercomputers.

These are the authors' claims, **not performance or production guarantees for Lance-graph**.

## Future Lance-graph comparison

- A **local aligned** thought-to-thought query over one 65,536-row Cartesian address universe is not the same problem as **distributed multi-variable** WCOJ. The former may collapse to a positional mask fold without trie, repartition, gather or join search.
- The paper is relevant where a query introduces different variable domains, cyclic relation bodies, distributed partitioning or recursive semi-naive fixpoints.
- The implemented GYO α-acyclicity test and cyclic negative fixture in [#1587](https://github.com/AdaWorldAPI/lance-graph/pull/1587) should anchor a source-independent parity comparison.
