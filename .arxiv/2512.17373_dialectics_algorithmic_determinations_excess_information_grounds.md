## Source-derived ideas

1. **Determinations / algorithmic parity:** Conceptual parts are represented within reversible consistency structures. For a determination, any missing component is reconstructible from the rest, allowing Kolmogorov-style logarithmic slack. This is an information constraint rather than a generic "thesis versus antithesis" metaphor.

2. **One-variable completion:** §3.3 Theorem 2 (arXiv v2) gives optimal auxiliary description length, up to stated slack, as max_i K(x_i | x_-i). It is an existence theorem, not an off-the-shelf inexpensive algorithm for arbitrary tuples.

3. **Excess information:** For a reversible split of experience I into P1 and P2, §§3.5/4 define the representation overhead Δ = K(P1) + K(P2) - K(I). A low-excess split reduces redundant/artificial description under the source's premises. Kolmogorov K is uncomputable in general; practical compression comparisons are proxies.

4. **Competing absorption of new patches:** A local dialectical move compares alternate *feasible* ways to reorganize concept descriptions, with conditional description costs. One cannot minimize a code length while ignoring reconstructibility or the information the split is required to retain.

5. **Pivots are not guaranteed:** §3.4 explains why not every rearrangement of determinations is feasible. Failure to reconcile is an admissible result.

6. **Grounds and communication:** §§4.2–4.3 introduce side information that anchors concept identities and can let a receiving agent recover a concept with a shared deterministic protocol. This does not establish that the receiving agent has correct causal or phenomenological understanding of the external world.

## Explicit nonclaims

- Compression is not independent verification of truth, causal origin, generalization, or moral/philosophical insight.
- Reconstructibility cannot turn duplicated evidence from 64 thoughts into 64 independent sources.
- Agreement or reversible encoding does not prove AI subjecthood or consciousness.
- Exact algorithmic complexity is not a practical runtime metric; measured compressor/MDL proxies can disagree.
- Forced Hegelian "synthesis" is not a mathematically warranted move when no feasible reconstruction exists.
