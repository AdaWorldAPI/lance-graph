# READ LATER: Can Aha Moments Be Fake? True Thinking Score and decorative CoT

**Authors:** Jiachen Zhao, Yiyou Sun, Weiyan Shi, Dawn Song.  
**Paper:** *Can Aha Moments Be Fake? Towards Quantifying Decorative and True Thinking in Chain-of-Thought*.  
**Primary source:** [arXiv:2510.24941v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24941v4) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.24941v4); revised 2026-05-26.  
**Status:** ABSTRACT REVIEWED / FURTHER READING QUEUED. Source abstract only; do not treat this as a complete-method reproduction.  
**Archived:** 2026-10-19. No code or production claim.

## Source abstract: what is reported

The paper proposes a **True Thinking Score (TTS)** to estimate the causal contribution of individual chain-of-thought steps to the model's eventual prediction, distinguishing causally influential steps from fluent but largely **decorative** ones.

Across 11 LLMs on reasoning benchmarks, the authors report a substantial proportion of low-TTS steps. The v4 abstract says more than 30% of Kimi-K2.6 steps on MATH score TTS <= 0.005. It also reports that removing 50% of lowest-TTS steps largely preserves accuracy, and self-training with pruned traces shortens reasoning by 66% while preserving performance on their specified Nemotron3-Nano-30B setup.

These figures refer to **the authors' experiments**, not to Lance-graph's 64 thoughts or its measured token/runtime usage. TTS is a paper-specific attribution proxy and is not identical to real-world causal identification.

## Lance-graph research questions (HYPOTHESES)

- Does deleting a proposed thought step actually alter predictions, derived facts, source dependencies or verified terminal outcomes?
- Can semantic Git's planned pickaxe/ablation distinguish a necessary premise from decorative narrative, redundant explanations and alternative sufficient proof paths?
- Does a high-confidence tautological restatement create a new independent evidential root? It must not.
- Can identical visible text conceal different internal computations? Compare a saved executable program/revision witness, not a story about reasoning.
- Are deletions measured on frozen held-out fixtures and exact prior versions, with independent negative controls?

**Boundary:** The paper studies LLM chain-of-thought. It neither implements nor validates the Lance-graph Thought Git API, and intervention on a trace does not prove that a token is a uniquely necessary cause of model behavior. No destructive deletion of repository/history is requested or allowed.

**Read later:** Examine the TTS intervention protocol, baseline and denominator, stability across repeated runs, counterfactual controls and alternate-proof overdetermination before transferring metrics.
