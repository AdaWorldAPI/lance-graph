# SOPD — Detached Revision Preserves Visited States

**Paper:** arXiv:2608.16333  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16333  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.16333

## Punchline

> **Learn from a counterfactual repair without letting the repair rewrite the trajectory that made it necessary.**

Step-Level On-Policy Distillation (SOPD) first lets the student complete its own trajectory, then asks the teacher for one coherent local continuation from each student-visited step boundary.

The useful transfer to lance-graph is not “teacher/student distillation” itself. It is the contract:

```text
observe real trajectory
        ↓
freeze visited states
        ↓
branch independent local repairs
        ↓
compare / accumulate evidence
        ↓
revise later
```

The repair is **detached** from the observed path. It may teach from a visited state, but it may not retroactively change which states were visited.

That gives a clean bridge between replayability and plasticity:

```text
observation != hypothesis != committed revision
```

---

## What the paper actually establishes

### 1. SOPD keeps student state occupancy while giving longer-horizon corrections

Standard on-policy distillation queries teacher logits at each student token. The supervision is on-policy, but once the student enters a bad trajectory, later teacher distributions are still conditioned on that increasingly bad prefix. The correction signal is therefore locally dense but fragmented.

SFT has the opposite property: the target sequence is coherent, but its prefixes come from the teacher rather than the student's visited states.

SOPD chooses the middle:

1. let the student finish the trajectory;
2. partition that trajectory into natural steps;
3. record the prefix before every student step;
4. from each recorded prefix, let the teacher generate exactly one coherent step;
5. train on those local teacher continuations.

Portable distinction:

```text
SFT:
teacher state -> teacher continuation

OPD:
student state -> token-local teacher distribution

SOPD:
student state -> coherent local teacher continuation
```

### 2. Teacher targets do not alter later student states

The full student rollout is fixed before teacher generation.

Therefore a teacher correction at step `k` cannot change the prefix used at step `k+1`.

Conceptually:

```text
student:
S0 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3 -> S4
      |     |     |     |
      T1    T2    T3    T4
```

Each `Tk` is conditioned on the real student prefix at that point, but no `Tk` is executed into the trajectory.

This is the strongest architectural harvest:

> **Revision evidence may branch from an observed state, but must not mutate the already-observed timeline.**

### 3. Correction granularity is itself an operator choice

The paper positions SOPD between two limiting cases:

- if the whole response is one step, supervision approaches sequence-level SFT;
- if a step shrinks toward one token, SOPD approaches token-level forward-KL OPD.

So the meaningful design axis is not merely “use a teacher” but:

```text
How large is the unit over which a correction is allowed to stay coherent?
```

For mathematics, the paper uses natural reasoning boundaries. For ALFWorld, one environment interaction turn is one step.

This makes step segmentation part of the learning algorithm, not presentation syntax.

### 4. Detached branches are parallelizable

Because the student trajectory is already fixed and every teacher target depends only on:

- the input;
- the corresponding student prefix;
- its own prior teacher tokens inside that one target;

the different step-level teacher branches do not depend on one another.

That means they can be produced independently or packed for efficient training.

Portable rule:

> **Independent counterfactuals should remain independent until an explicit fold/revision stage combines their evidence.**

### 5. Black-box supervision is sufficient

Vanilla OPD generally needs access to teacher logits.

SOPD only needs generated teacher responses.

That matters architecturally because the supervision contract can be expressed as ordinary observable continuations rather than privileged internal model state.

For lance-graph:

```text
candidate continuation
    >
hidden oracle internals
```

if the substrate can compare, score, replay, and revise candidates itself.

### 6. The empirical result says local repair can beat direct imitation

On the four reported mathematical reasoning benchmarks, the paper reports:

| Method | Avg4 |
|---|---:|
| Teacher | 46.0 |
| Student | 15.4 |
| OPD | 47.7 |
| **SOPD** | **57.7** |

SOPD therefore exceeds both the initial teacher and vanilla OPD in that setup.

On ALFWorld, the paper reports a 13.4 percentage-point average success-rate improvement over Vanilla OPD.

The transferable claim is limited:

> Better learning need not come from reproducing one globally superior teacher trajectory. It can emerge from accumulating coherent local repairs at the learner's own visited states.

This does **not** prove that any local-revision system will surpass its oracle.

# Harvest for lance-graph

## H1 — Make observed trajectory immutable to revision

For a replayable reasoning substrate:

```text
Trace = [S0, S1, ... Sn]
```

must remain observational fact.

A revision anchored at `Sk` may create:

```text
Candidate(Sk -> Ck)
```

but must not silently turn:

```text
[S0, ... Sk, Sk+1, ... Sn]
```

into:

```text
[S0, ... Sk, Ck, Ck+1, ...]
```

unless a new explicit replay/revision generation is started.

Possible conceptual split:

```text
ObservedTransition
DetachedCandidate
CommittedRevision
```

No new storage type is implied. The important part is the lifecycle.

---

## H2 — Revision should anchor to visited state, not an idealized state

A correction is most informative when it answers:

> Given exactly the state we actually reached, what locally coherent alternative was available here?

That is stronger than training only on ideal teacher trajectories because it samples the learner's own failure manifold.

For `revision.rs`:

```text
anchor = actual visited coordinate/state
candidate = local counterfactual continuation
evidence = comparison(candidate, actual)
```

This preserves a direct causal history of how the system arrived at the need for revision.

---

## H3 — 0..63 can own revision granularity

SOPD hard-codes domain-specific step boundaries.

lance-graph has a stronger possible abstraction: let the recipe decide the admissible correction horizon.

A recipe can determine:

```text
where a revision boundary exists
how much context is visible
how far a counterfactual may run
when evidence folds back
when a new state must materialize
```

That turns “step length” into deterministic orchestration semantics rather than text formatting.

Candidate interpretation:

```text
token / primitive op
    ↓
micro-recipe
    ↓
semantic operation
    ↓
compound 0..63 recipe
    ↓
whole trajectory
```

The useful level should be measurable rather than assumed.

---

## H4 — Detached candidates are evidence, not replacements

A candidate branch should initially mean:

```text
"another continuation from the same visited state"
```

not:

```text
"the correct continuation"
```

This keeps it compatible with the wider substrate:

```text
actual continuation
        vs
detached candidate
        ↓
agreement / disagreement
constraint closure
prediction error
causal support
provenance
entropy delta
        ↓
revision decision
```

Teacher authority can then be only one source of evidence among several.

---

## H5 — 59/60 can consume detached counterfactuals without becoming teacher bits

The useful connection to the epistemic-frontier work is:

```text
observed path exists
+
detached local alternative exists
+
alternative closes constraints the observed path leaves open
=
candidate epistemic pothole / missing link
```

So 59/60 should not encode “student wrong / teacher right”.

They can instead detect a sharper condition:

> **A locally available continuation would have restored expected closure that the observed path failed to restore.**

That remains below any 61..63 causal/epistemic grading.

---

## H6 — Rubikon should separate observation, counterfactual evaluation, and commitment

A clean pipeline is:

```text
run recipe
    ↓
record observed state
    ↓
spawn detached candidate(s)
    ↓
fold evidence
    ↓
Rubikon decision
    ├─ ignore
    ├─ keep unresolved
    ├─ schedule replay/exploration
    └─ commit into a new generation
```

This gives plasticity without corrupting replay.

# Testables

## T1 — Detached-revision invariant

Create a deterministic trace:

```text
T = [S0, S1, S2, S3, S4]
```

Then generate one or more counterfactual candidates from `S1..S3`.

### Expected

The serialized/hash representation of `T` is bit-identical before and after candidate generation.

### Falsifies

Any revision mechanism that mutates later observed states merely by evaluating an alternative.

---

## T2 — Prefix anchoring

At state `Sk`, deliberately create two different histories that happen to share a superficially similar local value but differ in semantically relevant prefix evidence.

### Expected

The candidate generator/revision recipe receives the actual bound prefix/state identity and may produce different candidates.

### Falsifies

A “local correction” API that is really detached from the state that caused the correction to be needed.

---

## T3 — Branch independence

Generate candidates for several anchors:

```text
C1 = revise(S1)
C2 = revise(S2)
C3 = revise(S3)
```

Run them:

1. serially in forward order;
2. serially in reverse order;
3. in parallel.

### Expected

For deterministic recipes:

```text
C1,C2,C3
```

are identical across schedules.

### Falsifies

Hidden cross-branch mutation or order dependence before the explicit fold stage.

---

## T4 — Granularity sweep

Solve the same deterministic puzzle/reasoning workload with revision boundaries at:

```text
primitive op
micro-recipe
semantic op
compound recipe
whole trajectory
```

Measure:

- terminal correctness;
- number of revisions;
- candidate bytes/tokens;
- fold count;
- materializations;
- replay size;
- false corrections;
- wall time.

### Hypothesis

There is a middle granularity where coherent local repair beats both extremely local fragmentation and whole-trajectory replacement.

### Falsifies

Treating one fixed granularity as architecturally privileged without evidence.

---

## T5 — Actual-vs-counterfactual closure

For every visited state `Sk`, compare:

```text
actual next transition
counterfactual candidate
```

against the same constraint set.

Record:

```text
closure(actual)
closure(candidate)
delta = closure(candidate) - closure(actual)
```

### Expected

Known injected mistakes produce positive local closure delta for at least one useful counterfactual.

### Falsifies

A revision score that cannot distinguish genuine repair from merely different continuation.

---

## T6 — No-oracle test

Replace the strong teacher with one of:

- a same-strength peer;
- a weaker model;
- a deterministic rule system;
- ontology constraints;
- local neighborhood proposals.

Keep the same visited-state anchoring and revision pipeline.

### Expected

If the substrate's value comes partly from scheduling and evidence combination, useful gains should survive at least some non-superior candidate sources.

### Falsifies

The claim that the architecture contributes learning beyond simply copying a stronger oracle.

---

## T7 — Bad-teacher containment

Inject deliberately wrong detached candidates at selected anchors.

### Expected

Wrong candidates:

- do not mutate the original trace;
- remain attributable to their source;
- can be rejected by closure/evidence checks;
- cannot become committed state without the normal revision gate.

### Falsifies

Any “teacher” path that bypasses substrate epistemics.

---

## T8 — 59/60 epistemic-pothole precision

Create a puzzle/graph with known missing relations.

At each visited state, generate detached alternatives and mark a pothole only when a candidate closes a previously unresolved constraint.

Measure:

```text
precision = useful-marked / all-marked
recall    = useful-marked / all-useful
```

Compare against:

- raw disagreement;
- random branching;
- entropy-only branching.

### Expected

Closure-aware detached branching should localize useful gaps better than disagreement alone.

### Falsifies

59/60 semantics that amount only to “another branch exists”.

---

## T9 — Commit starts a new replay generation

Take one accepted detached candidate and commit it.

### Expected

The original trace remains replayable as generation `G0`.

The repaired path begins as a distinct generation `G1`:

```text
G0: S0 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3
          |
          +-- detached C1

commit(C1)

G1: S0 -> S1 -> C1 -> ...
```

### Falsifies

Revision that destroys provenance by editing history in place.

---

## T10 — Candidate horizon ablation

From the same anchor, allow candidate continuations of different horizons:

```text
1 primitive
1 semantic step
2 semantic steps
until next materialization boundary
full suffix
```

### Measure

- correction utility;
- cost;
- branch divergence;
- replay burden;
- later closure.

### Expected

Longer is not automatically better.

There should be a measurable horizon after which extra counterfactual rollout buys little or begins to drift away from the anchored state.

### Falsifies

Assuming “more teacher continuation” is universally superior.

---

# Minimal substrate experiment

No model distillation is required.

Use a deterministic Sudoku / constraint graph with one injected wrong transition.

Record:

```text
S0 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3 -> S4
```

At `S1`, `S2`, and `S3` generate detached legal alternatives.

For every candidate compute:

- constraint closures gained/lost;
- remaining candidate population;
- agreement/disagreement against ontology/rules;
- whether a new materialization would be required;
- replay identity.

Then test:

```text
1. trace is unchanged after all branches
2. branch results are schedule-independent
3. the injected error creates a localized positive repair delta
4. wrong counterfactuals remain harmless
5. accepting one candidate creates a new generation, not edited history
6. granularity sweep exposes a measurable best repair horizon
```

This is enough to test the architectural harvest before introducing any new primitive.

# Guardrails

## Do not equate a detached candidate with truth

SOPD's teacher is treated as the source of supervision in the paper.

lance-graph should preserve the stronger distinction:

```text
candidate != truth
teacher != oracle
agreement != causality
```

A candidate becomes useful only through explicit evidence/revision logic.

## Do not let counterfactual generation contaminate observation

The central property is lost if generating or evaluating `Ck` changes `Sk+1`.

Observation must precede revision.

## Do not assume natural-language step boundaries are universal

The paper uses paragraph/“Step” style boundaries for mathematics and environment turns for ALFWorld.

For the substrate, step semantics should preferably come from the recipe/operator contract.

## Do not read “student beats teacher” as proof of self-improving truth

The result is empirical and setup-specific.

It supports the weaker, useful claim that local supervision at learner-visited states can outperform direct teacher imitation.

## Do not create a new revision subsystem before testing the existing fold/mask/replay machinery

The transferable requirement is lifecycle separation, not a particular class hierarchy.

# Architecture consequence

The clean harvest is:

```text
0..63 recipe
    ↓
observed trajectory
    ↓
immutable visited states
    ↓
detached local counterfactuals
    ↓
folded evidence
    ↓
59/60 closure / pothole detection
    ↓
61..63 epistemic grading where applicable
    ↓
Rubikon
    ↓
new generation only if committed
```

In compact form:

```text
Observation is history.
Counterfactuals are branches.
Revision is a decision.
Commitment creates a new history.
```

That is the piece worth carrying into lance-graph.
