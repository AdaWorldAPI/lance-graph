# Probabilities of Causation — Counterfactual Causation Can Be Bounded, Not Guessed

**Paper:** arXiv:1301.3898  
**Title:** Probabilities of Causation: Bounds and Identification  
**Authors:** Jin Tian, Judea Pearl  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.3898  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.3898

## Punchline

> **"Would Y have failed without X?" is a legitimate causal question even when the exact answer is not identifiable. The correct output can be a bound or an unresolved state, not fabricated certainty.**

Tian and Pearl formalize probabilities of necessary causation, sufficient causation, and necessity-and-sufficiency. They show how experimental and observational data can bound those quantities under stated assumptions.

For lance-graph, the portable lesson is epistemic discipline: counterfactual reasoning may be executable while the result remains only partially identified.

---

## What the paper actually establishes

The paper studies three related counterfactual quantities:

    PN   probability of necessity
    PS   probability of sufficiency
    PNS  probability of necessity and sufficiency

The central difficulty is fundamental: for one unit we cannot simultaneously observe the factual outcome and every counterfactual outcome.

The paper therefore asks what can be learned from combinations of observational and experimental distributions and derives sharp bounds under its assumptions.

Portable rule:

> **Counterfactual semantics and counterfactual identifiability are different questions.**

A system can know exactly what question it is asking while still being unable to certify one exact answer.

# Harvest for lance-graph

## H1 — Pearl input should not force Pearl output

Current CE64 already has a 3-bit CausalMask at bits 40..42:

    SO   association
    PO   intervention
    SPO  counterfactual
    SP   confounder check

That field can name the projection or question being executed.

It must not imply that the relation has earned a corresponding causal certification.

The result belongs elsewhere:

    input/query projection      -> CausalMask 40..42
    measured truth/evidence     -> frequency/confidence + receipts
    epistemic certification     -> EpistemicState5 59..63
    counterfactual branch tag   -> inference mantissa -6 where appropriate

## H2 — Unknown is a valid causal result

For an underidentified question:

    counterfactual requested
    evidence insufficient

the correct transition may be no certification change at all.

This fits the canonical EpistemicState5 product:

    topology may be known
    certification may remain Related / CausalCandidate

No synthetic promotion is required merely because a rung-3 query was executed.

## H3 — CUI BONO is candidate generation, not PN

The proposed CUI BONO loop sits before Pearl's necessity question:

    Indirect x Intermediate x Unknown x Related
            |
            v
    candidate beneficiary/intermediate b
            |
            v
    hydrate b and the proposed route
            |
            v
    counterfactual/interventional test
            |
            v
    bounded / identified causal evidence

Benefit proposes a candidate. It contributes no causal weight by itself.

## H4 — Overdetermination is the trap

If:

    A -> Y
    B -> Y

then removing A may leave Y unchanged even when A is genuinely causal.

Therefore:

> **Simple ablation is a dependency test, not a complete definition of causation.**

This matches the current P7a ruling that removal is not the causal certification path.

# Testables

## T1 — Identified necessity

Create a fixture in which intervention data plus structural assumptions identify a necessary factor.

Expected:

- counterfactual result is decisive;
- the result replays from the same evidence;
- no field outside the declared output changes.

## T2 — Underidentified necessity

Remove the evidence required to identify the counterfactual.

Expected:

    question still valid
    result not grounded / bounded
    no promotion to Causes

## T3 — Overdetermination

    Y = A OR B

Removing A while B is present should not be misread as proof that A has no causal role.

The system must preserve the distinction between:

- load-bearing for this realized path;
- causal under an intervention design;
- necessary in this unit/world.

## T4 — Association is not intervention

Construct Simpson reversal where SO and PO disagree.

Expected:

- SO observation may earn observational association;
- PO result remains separately measured;
- the observational population cannot overwrite an intervention-certified result.

## Guardrail

This paper supports formal counterfactual quantities and bounded identification.

It does not say that every graph deletion is a valid causal intervention, or that a beneficiary is a cause.

For the substrate:

> **Pearl's rung should select the reasoning operator; evidence should determine the certification.**
