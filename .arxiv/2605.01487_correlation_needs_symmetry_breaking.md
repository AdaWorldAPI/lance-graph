# arXiv:2605.01487 — Correlation needs symmetry breaking

**Paper:** Andrei Tokovinin, Brian D. Mason, Rene A. Mendez, Edgardo Costa, *Speckle Interferometry at SOAR in 2024 and 2025*  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01487  
**Harvest scope:** local correlation, propagation, coherence boundaries, epistemic provenance  
**Status:** substrate takeaways, not an optics implementation proposal

## Punchline

When the medium destroys a directly usable phase/state, do not reconstruct the full state first.

Fold many short, locally coherent observations into invariants, and materialize only where symmetry has to be broken or where the transfer function changes.

For lance-graph, the interesting lesson is not astronomy itself:

> **symmetric evidence can establish relation, but cannot manufacture directionality.**

That gives a concrete algebraic boundary between association, directional evidence, and causal claims.

---

## What the paper actually gives us

HRCam records two short-exposure cubes per target, typically 400–600 frames at 25 ms. Each cube is reduced into products including a power spectrum and a high-pass-filtered speckle autocorrelation function (ACF).

The reduction therefore preserves useful relational structure without first reconstructing a single clean long-exposure image.

The paper also exposes several epistemically useful boundaries:

- very short observations preserve information that a long exposure would smear;
- some instrumental/telescope disturbances are non-stationary across nearby cubes;
- autocorrelation-derived orientation can retain a 180° quadrant ambiguity;
- below nominal diffraction resolution, model-based estimates are possible but explicitly marked as less accurate;
- calibration is a separate error source and improved calibration models can revise prior measurements.

Those are the transferable pieces.

## Takeaway 1 — Auto-correlation is a fold, not a graph walk

For a binary population mask (M), the discrete analogue is:

[
C(\Delta) = \operatorname{popcount}(M \land \operatorname{shift}(M, \Delta))
]

For a Moore neighborhood:

[
\Delta \in \{N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW\}
]

this yields an 8-component local resonance signature without materializing edges or a neighborhood graph.

Conceptually:

```text
mask
  -> shift(delta)
  -> AND
  -> popcount
```

This is a candidate substrate primitive for asking:

> which local spatial/semantic relation is already implicit in this population?

It is especially relevant to missing-link / spatial-Sudoku work around bits 59/60: detect an expected relational pattern from population overlap rather than enumerate candidate triples.

### Critical invariant

Autocorrelation is symmetric:

[
C(\Delta) = C(-\Delta)
]

Therefore it can support:

> A and B co-occur in a stable relation.

It cannot, by itself, support:

> A causes B, or A points to B.

That epistemic ceiling should be encoded, not merely documented.

## Takeaway 2 — Direction requires a symmetry breaker

The paper's 180° ambiguity is a concrete reminder that symmetric transforms can destroy orientation information.

For the reasoning band this suggests an algebraic ladder:

```text
same-state symmetric evidence
        |
        v
association / resonance
        |
        | symmetry-breaking observation
        v
directional evidence
        |
        | temporal / interventional / counterfactual constraint
        v
causal claim
```

The exact 61..63 assignment remains architectural policy, but the guardrail is stronger than any naming scheme:

> **A symmetric fold must never, alone, promote evidence into a directed causal state.**

This is a useful invariant for CausalEdge64 regardless of final bit semantics.

## Takeaway 3 — Cross-time correlation is the minimal propagation operator

This is a substrate extrapolation from the paper, not a claim made by the authors.

Given two consecutive population states:

[
M_t,\; M_{t+1}
]

define:

[
W_t(\Delta)
=
\operatorname{popcount}
\left(
M_t \land \operatorname{shift}(M_{t+1}, \Delta)
\right)
]

Unlike autocorrelation, in general:

[
W_t(\Delta) \neq W_t(-\Delta)
]

so direction can emerge from temporal asymmetry.

A local propagation estimate is then:

[
\Delta^* = \arg\max_\Delta W_t(\Delta)
]

For Moore-8 this is just eight shifts, eight ANDs, and eight popcounts.

No explicit wavefront or edge population needs to be materialized.

Possible shape:

```text
population(t)
   |
   +-- shift N  -- AND population(t+1) -- popcount
   +-- shift NE -- AND population(t+1) -- popcount
   +-- shift E  -- ...
   ...
   +-- shift NW

              |
              v
      propagation signature
```

This may be a much cheaper primitive for local propagation than introducing a PDE-style wave model.

## Takeaway 4 — Coherence should define batch boundaries

The short-exposure strategy works because useful structure is captured while the transfer function is locally coherent.

The paper also describes quasi-periodic telescope jitter that can strongly affect one cube and disappear in another a few seconds later. A non-stationary disturbance cannot simply be calibrated away as though the same transfer function applied throughout.

Transfer to Rubikon / batched folds:

> **Fold aggressively while the local transfer function remains coherent. Break the batch when the observation regime changes.**

Not:

```text
1,000,000 operations -> one arbitrary giant fold
```

but:

```text
coherent epoch
  fold fold fold fold
        |
        | drift / discontinuity
        v
 checkpoint / generation++
        |
 new coherent epoch
```

This gives a possible principled meaning to a materialization boundary.

## Takeaway 5 — Uncertainty has local and calibration components

The paper separates internal measurement error from a calibration-dependent term.

The general substrate lesson is:

[
E_{prop}^2
=
E_{local}^2
+
E_{coordinate}^2
]

with the coordinate/calibration contribution allowed to grow with propagation distance or transform depth.

Do not repeatedly copy a scalar confidence through a long chain as if coordinates were perfect.

This may provide a better basis for epistemic attenuation than an arbitrary decay constant.

---

## Takeaway 6 — Inference below resolution must preserve provenance

The paper can estimate some binaries closer than the nominal diffraction limit by modeling target and reference-star power spectra. Those measurements are explicitly marked as less accurate.

Substrate rule:

> **Inference may increase effective resolution, but inferred resolution must not erase its provenance.**

The same semantic value can have different epistemic origins:

```text
observed
reconstructed
inferred
```

Those must remain distinguishable through revision.

---

## Takeaway 7 — Replayable evidence beats frozen conclusions

Improved calibrator models allow old measurements to be re-evaluated.

That maps directly to replayable knowledge:

```text
raw / sufficient-statistic evidence
+ calibration generation
        |
        v
 deterministic replay
        |
        v
 revised result
```

For evidence folds, retain enough sufficient statistics to support later revision without retaining or re-reading every original event.

# Testables

## T1 — Autocorrelation symmetry

For every supported displacement:

```rust
assert_eq!(acf(mask, delta), acf(mask, -delta));
```

Equivalent property test over random masks.

**Falsifies:** the proposed operator if implementation details introduce directional information where the algebra says none exists.

---

## T2 — Symmetric evidence cannot raise directed causality

Construct a state where only same-state autocorrelation evidence is available.

Run the full evidence/revision path.

Assert that no directed CausalEdge / reasoning-band promotion occurs without an explicit symmetry-breaking source.

Pseudo-contract:

```text
symmetric_evidence_only
    => association may increase
    => directed/causal level must not increase
```

**Falsifies:** any reasoning-band implementation that manufactures arrow direction from symmetric support.

---

## T3 — Cross-time propagation recovers a known translation

Generate:

[
M_{t+1} = \operatorname{shift}(M_t, \Delta_0)
]

for each Moore direction (Delta_0), plus controlled noise.

Compute (W_t(\Delta)).

Require:

[
\arg\max_\Delta W_t(\Delta)=\Delta_0
]

within a declared noise envelope.

**Falsifies:** the claim that the cheap shift/AND/popcount operator is sufficient as a local propagation signature.

---

## T4 — Static states do not invent direction

For:

[
M_{t+1}=M_t
]

the propagation operator must not report a confident non-zero direction merely because the population has anisotropic geometry.

This separates static spatial structure from actual temporal movement.

**Falsifies:** a propagation estimator that confuses shape with motion.

---

## T5 — Coherence boundary prevents invalid fold merging

Create two microbatches (A) and (B) from different transfer regimes.

Let (S(A)) and (S(B)) be cheap signatures and define a divergence (D).

When:

[
D(S(A), S(B)) > \epsilon
]

require:

```text
merge(A, B) == refused
or
generation(A) != generation(B)
```

and show that folding each coherent epoch separately reconstructs the reference result better than one merged fold.

**Falsifies:** the claim that coherence-triggered boundaries are useful rather than decorative metadata.

---

## T6 — Replay under revised calibration

Persist the minimal sufficient statistics plus calibration generation (g_0).

Apply a revised calibration (g_1).

Require that deterministic replay:

1. changes the derived result when the calibration truly differs;
2. leaves raw/sufficient-statistic evidence unchanged;
3. records that the new result derives from (g_1);
4. does not require re-reading the original full event stream.

**Falsifies:** the proposed evidence/revision separation if conclusions cannot be revised independently of raw ingestion.

# Minimal implementation candidates

Do **not** add an optics subsystem.

If experiments justify code, the smallest useful candidates are:

```text
AutoResonance
    C(delta) = popcount(M & shift(M, delta))

PropagationCorrelation
    W(delta) = popcount(M_t & shift(M_t1, delta))

CoherenceBoundary
    compare compact signatures of consecutive batches
    -> continue fold | generation break
```

Before introducing any new primitive, first prove these shapes cannot be expressed by existing mask/fold/rotation/coordinate machinery.

The desired outcome is an algebraic contract, not another subsystem.

**Correlation can survive destroyed phase, but direction requires broken symmetry; temporal asymmetry is the cheapest candidate bridge from local resonance to propagation.**
