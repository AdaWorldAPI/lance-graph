# TimePNS — Necessity Is Measured by Counterfactual Disruption

**Paper:** arXiv:2607.21573  
**Title:** Beyond Sufficiency: Time Series Explanation with Counterfactual Necessity  
**Authors:** Hongnan Ma, Yiwei Shi, Mengyue Yang, Weiru Liu  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21573  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.21573

## Punchline

> **A factor is decision-critical when intervention on it disrupts the original result, not merely when its presence can support that result.**

TimePNS is a necessity-aware explanation framework for time-series classifiers. It combines a sufficiency-oriented explanation mask with counterfactual interventions that measure whether candidate temporal factors are necessary.

---

## What the paper actually establishes

The motivating failure mode is simple:

    factor supports prediction

does not imply:

    prediction depends on factor

TimePNS therefore uses a two-stage design:

1. learn a causal generative process plus an initial sufficiency-oriented mask;
2. intervene on temporal factors and use prediction disruption as a necessity signal to refine the explanation.

The paper reports improved identification of decision-critical subsequences and improved sufficiency/necessity trade-offs on synthetic and real-world benchmarks.

# Harvest for lance-graph

## H1 — Counterfactual reaction is a first-class measurement

For candidate B:

    baseline = F(G)
    edited   = F(G under intervention on B)
    reaction = distance(baseline, edited)

Possible readouts already available or adjacent to the substrate include:

- verdict flip;
- population delta;
- route loss;
- fold-result delta;
- frequency shift;
- Fisher-Z shift;
- entropy change.

The reaction should be preserved as evidence, not compressed immediately into a causal label.

## H2 — Supports and necessary remain different

A candidate can be:

- supportive but redundant;
- necessary but not sufficient;
- both;
- neither.

The current canonical P7a certification should remain the authority for promotion.

## H3 — CUI BONO belongs before necessity

TimePNS assumes a factor is available to test.

Our proposed pipeline adds a candidate-generation stage:

    IndirectUnknown x Related
            |
            v
    beneficiary/intermediate proposal
            |
            v
    hydrate candidate B
            |
            v
    necessity / intervention / counterfactual tests

Thus:

> **CUI BONO chooses a hypothesis to attack; the attack supplies evidence.**

# Testables

## T1 — Supportive but redundant

Two routes support the same target.

Ablating either alone should leave the verdict intact.

## T2 — Necessary dependency

A unique mediator path should produce a decisive disruption under an admissible intervention.

## T3 — Spurious beneficiary

A node correlated with or benefiting from the outcome but absent from the mechanism should show little or no controlled reaction.

## T4 — Candidate-generator leakage

The candidate generator must not read the answer label or causal certification it is later asked to predict.

Shuffle the candidate bindings while keeping low-order statistics and verify the necessity signal collapses.

---

## Guardrail

Counterfactual disruption is evidence of decision dependence under the tested intervention.

It is not, by itself, proof of real-world agency or causation.

For lance-graph:

> **Candidate -> intervene -> measure reaction -> revise, never candidate -> cause.**
