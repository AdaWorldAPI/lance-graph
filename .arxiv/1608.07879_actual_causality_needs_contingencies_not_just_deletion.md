# Actual Causality and Responsibility — Contingencies Matter More Than One Deletion

**Paper:** arXiv:1608.07879  
**Title:** Causality and Responsibility for Formal Verification and Beyond  
**Author:** Hana Chockler  
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.07879  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.07879

## Punchline

> **Actual causality asks which factors become pivotal under admissible contingencies; responsibility grades how much surrounding context must change before a factor becomes pivotal.**

The paper surveys applications of Halpern-Pearl actual causality and causal responsibility to formal verification, counterexample explanation, coverage, symbolic trajectories, and legal reasoning.

For lance-graph the important harvest is the cure for naive ablation: one failed deletion test does not settle causal responsibility when redundancy or overdetermination exists.

---

## What the paper actually establishes

Halpern-Pearl-style actual causality does not stop at:

    remove X
    did Y change?

It allows a candidate cause to be evaluated under contingencies that hold or alter other variables.

The degree of responsibility then quantifies how much contingency is required before the candidate becomes pivotal.

Portable distinction:

    direct but-for dependency
    !=
    actual cause under redundancy
    !=
    degree of responsibility

This family of ideas has been useful in formal systems because it turns "why did this bad trace happen?" into a structured search over decisive conditions.

# Harvest for lance-graph

## H1 — One-at-a-time removal is only the first probe

Current dismech_counterfactual asks whether one cut is load-bearing for a thresholded verdict.

That is valuable, but a redundant mechanism can be causal without being individually load-bearing.

Next-level probe:

    candidate A
    +
    contingency mask C
            |
            v
    cut A under C
            |
            v
    does verdict become pivotal?

## H2 — Responsibility can be a search cost, not another CE64 field

Do not allocate bits for responsibility.

A compact external measure can be:

    responsibility(A) ~ inverse minimal contingency size

or another explicitly chosen score.

The register should continue to carry the edge and its epistemic state; the contingency search belongs in the planner/explainer layer.

## H3 — CUI BONO candidates can be ranked by falsifiability, not suspicion

If several hidden intermediates are proposed, prioritize candidates whose admissible contingency tests are cheap and discriminative.

That turns:

    who benefits?

into:

    which candidate gives us the strongest falsifiable counterfactual next?

without treating benefit as evidence.

# Testables

## T1 — Simple but-for cause

One path, no redundancy.

Cut the candidate and the verdict flips.

Expected responsibility: maximal under the chosen policy.

## T2 — Two redundant causes

    Y = A OR B

Neither single deletion flips Y while the other remains.

Under contingency B=off, A becomes pivotal; symmetrically for B.

The engine must not conclude "A is non-causal" from the first deletion alone.

## T3 — Irrelevant beneficiary

Add candidate C that benefits from Y but is absent from every causal route.

No admissible small contingency should make C pivotal without inventing a new mechanism.

## T4 — Minimal contingency search

If A becomes pivotal after one contextual cut and D requires three, the reported responsibility ordering should reflect the declared metric and replay deterministically.

---

## Guardrail

Responsibility is not blame, intent, motive, or moral judgment.

For lance-graph it is most useful as:

> **How much of the surrounding graph must change before this candidate becomes decision-critical?**
