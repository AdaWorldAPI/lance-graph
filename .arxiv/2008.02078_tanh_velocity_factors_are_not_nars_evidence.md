# arXiv:2008.02078 — Tanh velocity factors are not NARS evidence

**Paper:** Mahesh Chandra, *A Novel Method for Scalable VLSI Implementation of Hyperbolic Tangent Function* (2020; IEEE Design & Test, 2021).
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02078
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.02078
**Journal DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/MDAT.2021.3063308
**Companion:** [Singh (2024), tanh activation](2024-11-10_singh_tanh_activation_is_not_confidence.md)
**Comparison sources:** Pei Wang, [NARS 4.1](https://cis.temple.edu/~pwang/Publication/NARS-41.pdf); Isaev & Hammer, [NARS AGI-20 tutorial](https://www.opennars.org/workshop2020/NARS2019_files/NARS_Tutorial_AGI-20.pdf), especially slides 11–13 and 19.
**Harvest status:** research distillation and falsification plan; not approved implementation semantics or a measured CPU optimization.

## Punchline

> Tanh has a genuine hardware-friendly compositional identity: encode addition of latent arguments as multiplication of positive velocity factors. But the atanh of a cosine score is **not** the number of supporting NARS observations, and summing atanh values is **not** Pei Wang's frequency/confidence revision.

The valuable transfer is a bounded, quantizable **numerical transformation** and its testable fixed-point implementation. Keep the **evidence count** and **evidence provenance** independent of numerical representation.

## What the paper actually establishes

### 1. Hyperbolic addition has an exact multiplicative transform (pp. 2–3, equations 2, 4–6, 9–10)

For x = tanh(a) and y = tanh(b):

~~~text
tanh(a+b) = (x+y) / (1+x*y)
q(a) = (1 - tanh(a)) / (1 + tanh(a)) = exp(-2*a)
q(a+b) = q(a) * q(b)
tanh(a) = (1 - q(a)) / (1 + q(a))
~~~

The article first introduces the reciprocal velocity factor (1+x)/(1-x), then inverts it so positive arguments are represented by a factor q in (0,1), simplifying fixed-point range management. The multiplication identity is exact for real arithmetic; fixed-point rounding and reciprocal approximation are not.

This is closely related to inverse tanh/log-odds identities. **It does not assert a statistical distribution, a sample size, or a belief revision law.**

### 2. The proposed implementation is explicitly an accuracy/area/latency trade (pp. 3–4)

For tanh on signed fixed-point inputs, the paper:

- uses odd symmetry, absolute input and a final sign restoration;
- decomposes an input into weighted bit positions and multiplies precomputed velocity factors;
- groups bit positions into small LUTs (example: four 16-entry LUTs and three multipliers for the s3.12 example);
- deliberately shuffles LUT-address bit positions to reduce intermediate underflow/precision pressure;
- uses a reciprocal approximation (Newton–Raphson) to recover tanh from q;
- presents Verilog synthesis PPA configurations at different pipeline depths.

Its Table II reports maximum errors for the specific s3.12-input/s.15-output setup: 4.32e-5 for a three-stage Newton–Raphson configuration with one's-complement subtraction versus 4.44e-5 for the given reference conversion. These are **specific implementation/precision conditions**, not a bound for our palette256, AVX2 code, or NARS.

**Historical caveat:** The arXiv record includes an administrator's text-overlap notice referencing arXiv:2007.11976 and 2007.13516; do not make priority/novelty claims from this distillation.

### 3. The paper does not evaluate NARS, Fisher correlation, graph paths or noise gates

No NARS truth-value semantics, 3-sigma inference cutoff, independent-source accounting, or SIMD speedup is established by this paper. Those are separate hypotheses that need separate oracles and tests.

## Important non-equivalences in the proposed Google draft

| Claimed equivalence | Mathematical status | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| cosine(S,P) = NARS frequency | Not generally valid | Semantic similarity requires empirical calibration to evidence labels; one cosine is not an observation count. |
| atanh(cosine) = NARS total evidence w | False | atanh transforms a bounded value to an unbounded coordinate. It contains no sample size. |
| sum(atanh(s_i)) = NARS revision | False | It implements tanh addition of latent arguments, not a weighted sum of positive/negative evidence. |
| tanh(abs(z)) = NARS confidence c | False | It depends on magnitude/polarity; NARS confidence depends on **amount of evidence**. |
| 3 * 1.03/sqrt(N-3) = universal exact Gaussian gate | False | Approximate Fisher-z sampling variance needs a defined statistic, samples, model, null and dependency assumptions. |
| 3 sigma eliminates 99.73% noise/hallucinations | False | 99.73% is a normal-distribution central coverage value under stated assumptions, not classification accuracy or a hallucination guarantee. |
| L2 normalization provides Gaussianity | False | Norm-one vectors give cosine via dot product, not a normal sampling distribution. |
| tanh always remains < 1 in f32 | False in finite precision | A correctly rounded numerical implementation can return exactly 1.0 for sufficiently large finite arguments. |

### Counterexample A: evidence amount cannot be reconstructed from a similarity alone

NARS convention from the cited tutorial (k = 1):

~~~text
w_plus  = number/weight of positive evidence
w_minus = number/weight of negative evidence
w       = w_plus + w_minus
f       = w_plus / w                   (w > 0)
c       = w / (w + 1)
~~~

A score of f=0.5 could arise from (w_plus,w_minus)=(1,1) or (500000,500000). The frequency and the transformed signed score 2f-1 are identical; confidence differs drastically:

~~~text
(1,1):             w=2,       f=.5, c=2/3
(500000,500000):   w=1000000, f=.5, c≈.999999
~~~

The proposed c=tanh(abs(atanh(2f-1))) is zero in both cases. That is incompatible with NARS.

### Counterexample B: direct addition of Fisher coordinates disagrees with revision

Two independent, **disjoint** equal-weight evidence sets have frequencies f1=.9 and f2=.4, hence signed biases b1=.8 and b2=-.2:

~~~text
NARS revision:      f=(f1*w1 + f2*w2)/(w1+w2)=.65
NARS signed bias:   b=2*f-1=.30

Proposed z addition:
  z=atanh(.8)+atanh(-.2)
  b'=tanh(z)=(.8-.2)/(1-.16)=5/7≈.7142857
  f'=(1+b')/2=6/7≈.8571429
~~~

The transformed formula preserves the **hyperbolic tangent addition identity**, not the evidence-weighted average. Consequently it cannot be used as a drop-in Pei Wang revision rule.

### Counterexample C: the example labels signal and noise backward

In the supplied Python test, S=[.5,.5,.5,.5]:

~~~text
P_rauschen=[.49,.51,.50,.50]      cosine≈0.9999000
  palette256 index=254; z≈2.70886 -> gate OPEN

P_signal=[-.5,.5,-.5,.5]         cosine=0
  palette256 index=127; z≈-0.003918 -> gate CLOSED

N_horizon=150: 3*1.03/sqrt(147)≈0.254859
~~~

Near-identical vectors are labeled "noise" by the draft but pass its gate; orthogonal vectors labeled "signal" are suppressed. This is not a small precision bug. It shows the statistic and null hypothesis have not been defined.

### Counterexample D: Fisher-z does not magically make a distribution Gaussian

For a Pearson sample correlation r, under suitable iid/bivariate-normal assumptions and non-extreme population correlation, atanh(r) has an **approximately** normal sampling distribution with standard error ≈1/sqrt(n-3). Spearman may require different approximations or resampling; 1.03/sqrt(n-3) is not a universal law.

Even when Fisher combination is appropriate, pooling *estimated correlations* normally uses a precision-weighted **mean** of Fisher-z values, not their raw sum:

~~~text
z_pooled = sum_i((n_i - 3)*atanh(r_i)) / sum_i(n_i - 3)
r_pooled = tanh(z_pooled)
~~~

A sum of valid **log-likelihood ratios** is a different, defensible model under conditional-independence assumptions. Neither model is automatically NARS revision. Correlated graph paths can double-count common ancestors even if the arithmetic itself is exact.

# Harvest for lance-graph

## H1 — Encode evidence with sufficient statistics, not a single transformed score

Treat each S-P statement as an identity-bound record with at least:

~~~text
relation_id / S-P key
positive_weight (w_plus)
negative_weight (w_minus)
provenance/source-set or disjointness witness
optional feature/cosine diagnostic
optional quantized display score
~~~

Minimal algebra for independent/disjoint support:

~~~text
merge((p1,n1),(p2,n2)) = (p1+p2, n1+n2)
f = p/(p+n)        if total > 0
c = (p+n)/(p+n+k)  with explicit k > 0 (e.g. 1)
b = (p-n)/(p+n) = 2*f-1
~~~

All sums require checked bounds/saturation policy and evidence-disjointness validation. In particular, graph traversal, anaphora pointers, inherited edges and alternative reasoning paths must not be treated as independent new samples by default.

**If w_plus and w_minus are retained, revision is already cheap integer/fixed-point addition**. A denominator/re-normalization can be performed at an explicit materialization boundary, using a reciprocal LUT or certified arithmetic if profiling justifies it.

## H2 — Make velocity-factor/tanh a numerical codec, not belief semantics

Candidate independent transform API:

~~~text
SignedScalar -> TanhApprox -> QuantizedScalar
SignedScalar -> VelocityFactor -> TanhReconstruction
QuantizedScalar -> AtanhApprox (finite interior only)
~~~

Each has a domain, saturating/sentinel behavior, representation scale, error envelope and separate oracle.

Possible use cases include bounded activation, signed diagnostic display, compact neural mapping or a different explicitly-defined evidence model. A proof of tanh identities does not authorize relabeling them as confidence.

## H3 — Palette256 can be a legitimate bounded representation, not a free inverse

- 256 linearly spaced values on [-.999,.999] exclude exact zero (even number of bins); index-floor mapping introduces a signed bias.
- Both quantization error and inverse-tanh slope grow troublesome near ±1; define clamp boundaries and report max/percentile *round-trip* error.
- For a cosine-to-z transform, the lookup input is quantized but accumulating/fusing z may still require wider arithmetic.
- Chandra's nibble-grouped velocity-factor LUT is a distinct candidate from a 256-entry atanh LUT; compare both under equivalent error budgets.
- A zero-cost lookup is not established: memory latency, cache residency, indexing, quantization, norm and dot-product costs all matter.

## H4 — 3-sigma may gate a defined measured residual, never evidence by analogy

Accept a 3-sigma gate only when the signal is a **specified test statistic** T with an empirically or theoretically justified null variance. One safe research shape:

~~~text
null: no change in an already-bound S-P relation
T:   calibrated change in an observable statistic between epochs
sigma: measured per-epoch uncertainty, including quantization
gate: |T - E_null[T]| > 3*sigma
~~~

Test false-positive rate on null data, power under injected effects, multiple comparisons, heteroskedasticity, correlated paths, and false suppression of real but small effects. If the uncertainty model is unavailable, leave gate inactive and use deterministic quantization-error bounds for **numerical** denoising instead.

Do not hard-zero stored evidence because a provisional test is below threshold; retain sufficient statistics for replay and future accumulation.

## H5 — Repair the supplied Rust/SIMD experiment before interpreting performance

The pasted code is **not** production-ready:

1. _mm256_maddubs_epi16 uses unsigned bytes for the first operand and signed bytes for the second, with **signed saturation** in the resulting i16 pairs. Passing arbitrary signed i8 for both inputs computes the wrong dot products and norms, especially for negative values.
2. For signed i8 × signed i8, explicitly widen both operands to i16 and use appropriate multiply/add with non-saturating, overflow-checked accumulation, or a proven equivalent identity. Benchmark the corrected implementation against a scalar oracle.
3. Verify equal nonempty slice lengths, nonzero norms and accumulator width. zip silently truncates mismatched lengths in the scalar fallback; normalization can divide by zero.
4. The supplied first phase already performs floating-point sqrt and division. Runtime LUT filling calls ln and tanh. The claim "no floating point in core" is false as written.
5. The claimed no_std compatibility, runtime AVX2 detection, target-feature annotations and compiler inlining require an actual compile/test matrix, including a non-AVX2 fallback. An inline attribute is not a performance guarantee.
6. "Confidence" does a **linear scan of 256 z entries for every passing inference**; this may dominate the alleged constant-time lookup advantage.
7. i8 re-biasing with XOR 0x80 can map signed bytes to unsigned indices, but cannot correct the preceding asymmetric [-127,+127] score quantization or the meaning of the LUT.
8. "32 inputs per CPU clock" and "16–24x speedup" are unmeasured claims. Vector width is not instructions-per-cycle, throughput or end-to-end speedup.
9. Quantization, clipping, float-to-int conversion, rounding mode and saturation must be tested against an exact scalar reference, including all-negative, all-positive, near-orthogonal and all-zero inputs.

## H6 — Placement in the cold vs hot architecture

~~~text
COLD / OFFLINE
 source harvest & mathematical specification
   -> independent NARS (p,n) reference oracle
   -> independent tanh/atanh/velocity-factor reference oracle
   -> egglog proof/search for semantics-preserving candidate rewrites
   -> certification, quantization error and perturbation suites
   -> R2IL/mask-risc recipe only after contract is certified

HOT / BOUND EXECUTION
 relation-key / source binding
   -> disjoint-evidence check / de-dup
   -> add sufficient statistics (p,n) using existing lanes
   -> optional calibrated mask gate for a named observable
   -> deferred normalization/display, only if requested
   -> deterministic replay and audit trail
~~~

Do not invent new substrate primitives before testing whether existing integer lanes, population masks, bounded LUTs, SoA views, R2IL folds and mask-risc execution already suffice.

# Testables and minimal experiments

## T1 — NARS revision differential (semantic hard gate)

Generate disjoint integer evidence counts p_i,n_i in a bounded domain; compare associative count addition against the NARS tutorial's f,c revision for all defined input cases (including zero evidence handling). Recheck source-overlap refusal and no double counting.

**Must pass** for any implementation claiming "NARS revision"; the atanh-sum candidate must fail as shown in counterexample B.

## T2 — Confidence depends on sample size

Hold f=.5, vary w across orders of magnitude; assert c increases according to w/(w+k). Hold w constant and vary f; assert c is unchanged. Opposite-polarity evidence can be abundant and confident.

**Falsifies:** any c=tanh(abs(z)) rule that calls itself Pei Wang confidence.

## T3 — Tanh codec error envelope

Sweep all 256 input indices plus dense continuous test points, including zero and near ±1:

~~~text
exact tanh and atanh
palette256 (floor vs nearest)
velocity-factor grouped-LUT scheme
piecewise-linear alternative / platform libm baseline
~~~

Measure absolute error, worst-case near endpoints, symmetry, monotonicity, interpolation error, float32 saturation and total time.

## T4 — Signed SIMD oracle and throughput

Property-test signed i8 dot/norm against scalar wide-integer reference, including all-negative vectors, alternating signs, 127/-128, non-multiple-of-32 tails, mismatched lengths and overflow boundaries. Then measure per-relation ns, cycles, and total pipeline including LUT/scans, not just inner MAC throughput.

## T5 — Statistical gate calibration

For a **named** statistic and null-generating process, collect false-positive rate, statistical power and empirical quantiles under dependency and quantization. Compare gated vs ungated replay. Never use the paper's tanh circuit results as evidence for 3-sigma statistical performance.

## T6 — Provenance duplication adversary

Present the same supporting graph observation through 2, 4, 8, 64 equivalent paths. Correct disjoint-evidence accounting must prevent c from increasing merely because the graph has more traversal routes.

## T7 — Recipe replay and proof boundary

Certify the exact fixed-point/float semantics of each chosen transform; freeze LUT bytes, rounding, feature dispatch and reduction order. Replay on a new S-P snapshot and verify against the reference and perturbation suite. If an egglog rewrite uses real-number algebra, provide a separate numeric-error certificate before treating it as valid for fixed-point execution.

---

## Decision

**Harvest Chandra's velocity-factor identity and grouped-LUT hardware strategy as candidate numerical codecs. Reject the proposed atanh-addition replacement for NARS revision. Preserve w_plus, w_minus, evidence independence and provenance as the ground truth; apply tanh only at a separately specified numerical boundary.**

The remarkable performance opportunity may be simpler than the draft: *do the legally additive evidence arithmetic directly in registers, and avoid decoding f/c at all until somebody requests it.*

No executable changes are implied by this research note.
